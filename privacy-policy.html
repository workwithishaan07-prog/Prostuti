Privacy Policy — Prostuti

Last updated: August 2026

What this app does

Prostuti is a study app for West Bengal government exam preparation, offering quiz practice, a study planner, and flashcards.

Data collection

Prostuti does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not use accounts, logins, analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

Data storage

Your quiz scores, planner tasks, and exam date are stored only on your own device, using your browser/app's local storage. This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data permanently removes it.

Permissions

Prostuti does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or any other device permissions.

Third-party services

This app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-processing services.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer at the email address listed on the Play Store listing.
